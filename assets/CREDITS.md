## Porsche 911 (930) Turbo and BMW M3 E36

- **Porsche:** “FREE 1975 Porsche 911 (930) Turbo” by Lionsharp Studios. Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/free-1975-porsche-911-930-turbo-8568d9d14a994b9cae59499f0dbed21e
- **BMW:** “BMW E36 M3” by Martin Trafas. Original: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/bmw-e36-m3-762b90efd3dc413cb61c3e3c8097b8ac
- Both are **CC BY 4.0**, https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Attribution and license are also embedded in each GLB's `asset.extras`. Selected upstream catalog records are retained in `models/additional-models-metadata.json`.
- Distribution: https://github.com/pedrosoandre/carros . Files `assets/porsche-911-930.glb` and `assets/bmw-e36-m3.glb`.
- Upstream processing includes vertex welding, geometry simplification, pruning, and meshopt compression.
- Runtime modifications: source display floors removed, Porsche duplicate coating shell removed, orientation/scale normalized, paint and glazing adjusted, charcoal cabin surface values, studio lighting. Porsche paint vertex normals are recalculated to soften source shading artifacts.

Thumbnails are renders of the corresponding models, generated by `scripts/inspect.mjs` and `scripts/inspect-collection.mjs`; the same model attribution applies.
